# Kami Circle

## Family, together.

**Kami Circle** helps families stay connected with private location sharing, places, arrival and departure updates, family and direct messages, and SOS when it matters.

### Built for people you trust

- Create your own private family circle.
- Invite only the people you choose.
- See family locations when they choose to share.
- Save important places such as Home, School or Work.
- Get arrival and departure updates.
- Use **Family Chat** or private one-to-one messages.
- Send an SOS alert to your family.
- Turn location sharing off at any time.

## Android

### Download Kami Circle

Use the latest Android release:

**[Download Kami Circle for Android](https://github.com/kilmane/kami-find-download/releases/latest/download/Kami-Find.apk)**

### Install

1. Tap **Download Kami Circle for Android**.
2. When the APK finishes downloading, open your phone's **Downloads** or **Files** area.
3. Tap **Kami-Find.apk**.
4. If Android asks whether to allow installation from this source, allow it for this installation.
5. Tap **Install**.
6. Open **Kami Circle**.

### Starting your own family

Create an account, choose **Create a Family Circle**, then invite the people you trust.

### Joining someone else's family circle

Create an account or sign in, choose **Join with Invite Code**, and enter the one-use code you received.

After joining, turn **Location Sharing** on. When Android asks for location permission, choose **Allow all the time** if you want your family to receive location updates while Kami Circle is closed or your phone is locked.

---

### Private by design

Kami Circle is designed around private family circles rather than public discovery. Location sharing is controlled by each member and can be switched off at any time.
